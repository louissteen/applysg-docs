## Office hours

The ApplySG product team holds open office hours every **Wednesday, 3–4 pm SGT**. Bring a form you are building; a solutions engineer will review it live. Book a slot from **Settings → Help → Office hours**.

## Status

Live platform status and scheduled maintenance windows are published at `status.apply.gov.sg`. Subscribe to email or RSS alerts from that page.
