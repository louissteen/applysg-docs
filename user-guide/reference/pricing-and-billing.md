# Pricing and Billing

📄 Reference · Updated: 1 Oct 2026 · Freshness: ✅ Current

ApplySG is a whole-of-government platform. Agencies are charged on a **cost-recovery** basis through the standard inter-agency billing arrangement; there is no procurement step.

## Plans

| | Starter | Standard | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Published forms | Up to 5 | Up to 50 | Unlimited |
| Submissions / month | 2,000 | 25,000 | Custom |
| Workspace members | 10 | 100 | Unlimited |
| Integrations (API clients) | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Multi-agency workflows | | ✅ | ✅ |
| Dedicated support channel | | | ✅ |
| **Monthly fee** | **S$0** | **S$1,200** | By agreement |

Overage is billed at **S$0.08 per submission** above the plan allowance.

All figures on this page are illustrative demo values.

## What counts as a submission

A submission is counted when an applicant selects **Submit** and the case enters the *Submitted* stage. Drafts, previews with test identities and resubmissions after a clarification request are **not** counted.

## Reading your statement

Statements are issued on the first working day of each month under **Settings → Billing**.

## Changing plan

Admins can request an upgrade from the Billing page; it takes effect from the next billing cycle. Downgrades require the workspace to be within the lower plan's limits.
